The documented pickup bypass is routed later, after Clawline and Faydown Cloak. [Forum guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3577460696) · [Bass% bypass](https://speedpastes.org/kXQvcbMl)
- G&Z roof duel route tested on the scout and Forum aid confirmed on the timed run. Exhaust the first Greymoor conversation, open the outer Songclave roof shortcut after the wind puzzle, leave, and return via that shortcut for the duel. Include the post-duel conversation and the aid offer at the Forum.
- Pair slot unlocks with actual tool pickups. Cogfly is the planned second red tool in High Halls (1 Craftmetal). Consider Weighted Belt at Mort's first pass for the yellow loadout; avoid spending time filling slots without a useful tool plan. Ascendant's Grip is a conditional Songclave option (350 rosaries, Jubilana must be available), not a confirmed purchase.
- Route Silk capacity as complete pairs. First pair is Atla + Greymoor. The Greymoor pickup worked on a late timed detour with the arena uncleared. Scout it with the earlier ability set. Lower Cogwork + eastern Underworks is a candidate second pair. Commit to each pair together. [Spool Fragment choices](./Silksong%20-%20Spool%20Fragment%20Candidates.md)
- Budget **220 rosaries for Mort's Tool Pouch** on the first Pilgrim's Rest pass and keep Loddie's Marrow target practice reward. Adding Mort's upgrade raises Cogfly capacity from **5 to 6** and Straight Pin capacity from **15 to 18**. Weighted Belt remains a separate option for 160 rosaries. [Tool Pouch capacities and sources](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Tool_Pouch)
- **Scout a second Crafting Kit.** Accept Crawbug Clearing in Bellhart after Widow. On the Bell #3 return, farm 25 Ragpelts from the two Craws on the Halfway Home roof, resetting them by going inside and back out. Turn the Ragpelts in to Creige and time the extra work. If that takes too long, budget 450 rosaries for the Twelfth Architect's kit after Clawline. The second kit raises Cogfly damage from 5 to 7 per hit. [Crawbug Clearing](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478208) · [Twelfth Architect](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Twelfth_Architect) · [Cogfly damage](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Cogflies)
- Open **Greymoor Bellway** on the first pass, then use it after Cling Grip to shorten the return toward Halfway Home and Bell #3. It is helpful but not required for the bypass.
- Skip Lumble's unreliable Dice minigame on Switch 2. Blasted Steps Bellway access made the timed run's later Dice return practical. Plan that access on the first pass if including the Act 2 pickup in a future run.
