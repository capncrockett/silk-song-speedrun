## 6. Corrections and carry-forward decisions

The primary mistake during this section was missing the Far Fields Mask Shard earlier in the run. Garmond expects Shellwood's shard to complete the sixth mask; ours brought us to 3/4 instead.

We agreed not to interrupt Shellwood to retrieve it. The revised plan was to obtain the next shard in Weavenest Atla after Widow, completing the sixth mask before Last Judge. For a fresh run, collecting Far Fields on the first pass restores Garmond's intended progression and avoids that recovery detour.

Our other broader route decisions remain in place: Hunter Crest, no Mount Fay or double jump, no Craw Lake Arena for Thread Storm or Three-fold Pin, and Last Judge rather than Sinner's Road and the Mist.

One unresolved detail needs special attention: Bell #3 in Greymoor. We planned to use Cling Grip to bypass Craw Lake, but that shortcut was not personally tested. Garmond's published guide instead routes through the arena and balloon platforming. We should treat the Cling Grip bypass as a separate route modification requiring verification, not a completed step.&#x20;
