Compared against Tay Bravo's Garmond Route and our customized Last Judge / Hunter / Greymoor Bell #3 Cling Grip route. Greymoor fragment **478263** is confirmed collected on Run #2. The selected six below document future route planning, not newly reported pickups on an existing file. [Garmond Route](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3577460696)

## User choices

- **Final selection:** Bone Bottom 478080, Atla 478230, Deep Docks 477926, Greymoor 478263, Grand Gate 478586, and eastern Underworks 478704. None adds a dedicated spool arena; the usual post-Clawline exit fight remains.
- **Greymoor pickup confirmed on Run #2.** On a late timed visit, the user found the balloons present at Craw Lake and collected 478263 with the arena uncleared. The documented Cling Grip requirement was rechecked November 9 and fits the earlier Bell #3 pass; the actual Run #2 record still confirms only the late pickup. [Greymoor spool](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478263)
- **Lower Cogwork Core (478618):** archived alternative, omitted from the final six.
- **Eastern Underworks / Cauldron (478704):** Joseph explicitly selected it for the final run on November 9. Arena 479347 rewards the separate central spool 478931; the eastern pickup is behind wall 480018 approached from the east.
- **Deep Docks (477926): selected.** Collect during the Forge Daughter visit. This is a future route decision, not a reported pickup on an existing file.
- **Bone Bottom / Mosshome (478080): selected.** Joseph confirmed he likes this pickup; collect it on the early town loop.
- Retain the skip recommendations for the separate central Underworks arena (478931) and double-jump-dependent High Halls fragment (478909).

## Settled pairs in collection order

1. **Bone Bottom 478080 + Atla 478230:** first completed spool after Widow and the Eva visit.
2. **Deep Docks 477926 + Greymoor 478263:** second completed spool on the Bell #3 return, before Judge.
3. **Grand Gate 478586 + eastern Underworks 478704:** third completed spool in Act 2.

All six give **+3 Silk capacity**. These are itinerary pairs; any two halves combine in-game. Collection timings and individual detours have not been measured. No new pickups have been recorded on Run #2 or the scout file.

## November 9 pin clarification

- Atla 478230 uses Needolin for entry and Cling Grip for the upper passage; the spool does not require Moss Mothers or Faydown Cloak.
- Bone Bottom 478080 has no rosary/ability cost, but includes the trapdoor, lift lever, and a boobytrapped corridor.
- Grand Gate 478586 is selected. It requires no double-jump and replaces the lower Core detour in the six-fragment plan.
- The dedicated central Underworks spool arena is 479347. The normal Cauldron exit arena after Clawline is 480025. Eastern spool 478704 is distinct from both.
