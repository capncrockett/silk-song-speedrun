## Pairs for the rerun

Route each two-fragment pair together. A single fragment does not add Silk capacity. Choose both pickups before spending time on either.

1. **Weavenest Atla + Greymoor:** first planned pair. Collect during the Eva / mask-shard visit and the Cling Grip return for Bell #3 after verifying the Greymoor approach with the earlier kit.
2. **Lower Cogwork Core + eastern Underworks (478704):** second proposed pair. Scout both branches before committing either to the timed route.
3. **Bone Bottom / Mosshome + central Deep Docks:** optional earlier pair if testing shows the trap and burning-floor rooms are quick and reliable.

Two fragments add one Silk capacity, so the first four proposed pickups would provide **+2 Silk capacity** once both pairs are complete. These are candidate additions, not a finalized route or a claim that they are free of detours.
